# Podcast Audio Fetcher

Up: [[Sources]]

**Status: deferred.**

Covers podcast appearances that aren't on YouTube (or where captions are
unavailable) — would need Whisper transcription of raw audio.

## Why deferred

Compute/time cost of transcribing audio is high relative to just pulling
existing captions via [[YouTube]], and most of Saladino's major podcast
appearances (JRE, Shawn Ryan, etc.) are already YouTube-available with
captions. Not worth building before the end-of-week deadline; revisit if
the corpus needs audio-only sources later.
